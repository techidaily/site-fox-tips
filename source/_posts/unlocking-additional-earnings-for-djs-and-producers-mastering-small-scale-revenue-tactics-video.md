That’s why it’s a good idea to diversify your portfolio and add additional revenue streams. As Joey Santos fittingly refers to them in the video above, “Micro Revenue Streams”.

Joey provides a few great DJ tips for bringing in additional income while on the rise to widespread recognition. From running a Go Fund Me type page, a virtual tip jar, and more.
