## User Created Skins Inspired by Denon DN-HC4500 for DJ Deck Controller: Step Into the Design Mode

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/denondex3skincoverimage.jpg?resize=573%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/denondex3skincoverimage.jpg?fit=573%2C339&ssl=1 "denondex3skincoverimage")

Our **[DEX 3 DJ mixing software’s](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** redesigned graphics engine supports user created skins utilizing our freshly updated “[**PCDJ** **Skin Designe**r](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)” – this means our users can select a different skin in DEX 3’s options to change the entire

look and feel of the software’s GUI (_Graphical User Interface_). DEX 3 already comes equip with all new [4-Deck](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [2-Deck](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) default skins at all (_high_) resolutions, each with a re-designed [video mixing tab](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) (now with _separate preview windows_), effects grid tab and  
sampler tab.

Our community is always looking for new user created and submitted skins we can include on PCDJ.com for download for DEX 3\. Different DJs have different needs and styles of course., so much of the time user submitted skins fill a niche that other DJs with similar needs gravitate to.

Our first user submitted skin comes to us from Emiliano Vargas (_DJ Vargas_) of Sweden. It’s a **1280 x 800 resolution skin** modeled after the wildly popular **Denon DN-HC4500** [DJ controller](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and features stacked waveform’s running horizontally across the top and rotating platters that mimic those on the HC4500\. Download the skin below and be sure to let Emiliano know what you think of his aptly named “Denon DEX 3 Skin” by [visiting his website and signing his guestbook](http://www.partydude.se)!
